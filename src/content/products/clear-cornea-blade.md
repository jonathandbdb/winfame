Designed for the main incision in phacoemulsification and micro-incision cataract surgery (MICS). The 4-edge, 8-bevel geometry gives a smooth entry and a self-sealing tri-planar architecture.

- 12 widths to match every phaco tip and IOL injector system
- 60° tip angle for controlled entry into the anterior chamber
- Consistent edge sharpness across the full range
