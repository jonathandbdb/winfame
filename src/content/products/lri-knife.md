Limbal relaxing incisions reduce pre-existing corneal astigmatism at the time of cataract surgery. A fixed-depth guard delivers a consistent, reproducible incision depth along the steep meridian, without intra-operative adjustment.

- Five pre-set depths to match nomogram recommendations
- Precision footplate for stable contact with the limbus
- Ergonomic handle
